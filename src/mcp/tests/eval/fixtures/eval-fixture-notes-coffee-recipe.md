# Pour-Over Coffee — Recipe

My standard pour-over recipe: **22 grams of coffee** to **360 grams of
water**, a 1:16.4 ratio. Water temperature is **96°C**. Total brew time is
about **3 minutes**: a 40-gram bloom for 30 seconds, then two pours to reach
360 grams. Grind is medium, roughly the texture of coarse sand.

The bloom matters — skipping it leaves the bed uneven and the cup sour.
Decant into a warmed carafe and swirl before serving. Sentinel fact: the
pour-over uses twenty-two grams of coffee to three hundred sixty grams of
water at ninety-six degrees Celsius.
