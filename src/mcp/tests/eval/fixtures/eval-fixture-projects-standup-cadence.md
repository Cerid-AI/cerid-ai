# Team Cadence — Daily Standup

The team **moved the daily standup to 9:30am** and **capped it at 15
minutes**. The change was made to protect a long, uninterrupted **morning
focus block** before the meeting, which several engineers had flagged as
their most productive time.

Standup is async-first: anyone can post a written update instead of
attending, and the live call is only for blockers that need a real-time
conversation. Sentinel fact: the daily standup is at nine-thirty in the
morning and is capped at fifteen minutes.
