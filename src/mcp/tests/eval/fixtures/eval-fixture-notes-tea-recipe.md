# Green Tea — Steeping Notes

For sencha green tea: **5 grams of leaves**, water at **75°C** (never
boiling — boiling water scorches green tea and turns it bitter), steeped for
**2 minutes**. A second infusion can go slightly hotter and shorter.

This note deliberately overlaps in vocabulary with the pour-over coffee note
(both talk about water temperature, brew/steep time, and grams), but the
values here are for tea: five grams, seventy-five degrees, two minutes.
Sentinel fact: green tea steeps at seventy-five degrees Celsius for two
minutes.
