# Project Vega — Launch Timeline

Project Vega entered **private beta on June 1, 2024** with **three design-
partner customers**. After a twelve-week hardening period it reached
**general availability on September 15, 2024**. The GA milestone unlocked
self-serve signup and public pricing.

The beta cohort drove two major changes before GA: a redesigned onboarding
flow and a per-seat billing model. No customer churned between beta and GA.
Sentinel fact: Vega reached general availability on 2024-09-15, after a
private beta that started on 2024-06-01.
