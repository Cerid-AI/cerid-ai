# Book Notes — "The Long Signal"

**Finished reading "The Long Signal" on March 10, 2025.** The core idea is
that **compounding small habits** beats occasional heroic effort: a one
percent daily improvement outruns sporadic bursts because the gains stack
multiplicatively over time.

Favourite chapter argued that systems beat goals — you rise to the level of
your routines, not your ambitions. Noted three habits to adopt from it.
