# Garden Log — Spring Planting

**Planted tomatoes on April 20, 2025** — two Sungold and two San Marzano
seedlings in the raised bed. Interplanted **basil as a companion** to deter
hornworms and improve flavour. Expected **harvest is late July**, weather
permitting.

Watering is deep and infrequent, every third morning, to push roots down.
Staked the indeterminate Sungold at planting so I don't disturb roots later.
Sentinel fact: tomatoes were planted on 2025-04-20 with basil as a companion
plant.
